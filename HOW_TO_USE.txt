===========================================
LABEL CROPPER - User Guide
===========================================

WHAT THIS APP DOES
------------------
Crop shipping labels from PDF files and print them.
Works with both normal and rotated PDF formats.


HOW TO USE
----------

1. LOAD A PDF
   - Option A: Paste a direct PDF URL and click "Load URL"
   - Option B: Click "Load PDF from File" to select a local PDF

2. SELECT CROP AREA
   - Click and drag on the PDF preview to draw a rectangle
   - The blue dashed box shows your selection

3. CROP & SAVE
   - Click "Crop & Save PDF" button
   - The cropped PDF is automatically saved

4. PRINT (Optional)
   - After cropping, click "Print" to open print dialog
   - Print the cropped label


WHERE ARE CROPPED FILES SAVED?
------------------------------
%APPDATA%\Label Cropper\label\

Files older than 24 hours are automatically deleted.


AUTO-UPDATES
------------
The app checks for updates on startup.
When available, you'll see a prompt to download and install.


VERSION
-------
Current version is displayed below the app title.


TIPS
----
- For best results, crop slightly inside the label borders
- Both portrait and landscape PDFs are supported
- The app handles rotated PDFs automatically
